Layouts and session controls

Every design starts with an overlay. Choose its layout, set its appearance, then save before adding it to your stream.

One overlay editor

Choose any of the nine overlay types from Studio’s menu. On mobile, open Menu to reach the overlays, Canvas Designer and account options.

  • Cards, where available, contain your gifts, actions and sounds.
  • Layout chooses the arrangement. The controls below it adapt to the selected layout.
  • Settings contains the overlay’s behaviour and rules.
  • Style changes colours and surfaces. Style presets, including Default, preserve your chosen layout.
  • Session controls appears on Community Combo, Crown Raid, Pick Our Path and One More Thing. Start, pause, reset or adjust a session from the same overlay editor.

Saving and discarding changes

Unsaved edits stay in the open editor only. Studio does not automatically save or restore drafts. When switching overlays or designs, choose Keep editing or Discard changes. Reloading or closing a browser tab may show the browser’s own unsaved-changes prompt. Revert to last save restores the latest saved design. Version history remains available.

Layouts retain your cards, triggers, sounds and colours. Transparent styles remove card backgrounds while keeping artwork, text and progress marks. Pick a preview background that makes your chosen colours easy to inspect; it does not change the actual transparent output.

During your stream

Save the settings before starting a session. Community Combo counts matching gifts within an inactivity window; Crown Raid uses gifts as damage; Pick Our Path counts the two selected gifts; One More Thing advances through your configured steps. In Session controls, Pause stops counting, Hide changes visibility, and Reset clears the current session. Manual corrections change session totals, not the saved design. A running session keeps its starting rules until a new session is started.

Multiple instances of the same saved design share a session. Duplicate the design for independent totals. Use one audible instance to avoid duplicated sound. Sample events stay in previews and never count toward your live totals.

Add to your stream

Save the design, then use Direct link for one overlay or Add to canvas to combine several. Add the private URL as a Browser Source in compatible streaming software, using the displayed source dimensions. Position it around your gameplay and leave space for the platform’s chat and controls.

In Studio, open TikTok LIVE, enter your username and choose Connect. Your Direct URLs and Canvases share this connection. If you enable the optional TikFinity backup, run TikFinity Desktop on the streaming computer and connect it to the same username. Ordinary browsing and previews do not need access to your local network. Keep source links private. Live overlay events can include viewer names and avatar URLs; session state expires after 24 hours.
