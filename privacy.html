Your data

Privacy, without the fog.

This notice explains what King Gumption Studio stores when you use the website, Studio accounts and output tools.

Who is responsible

King Gumption Studio is a personal project operated in the United Kingdom by Dominic Williamson. Dominic Williamson is the controller for account information and cloud overlay settings processed to provide this service.

Privacy contact: Contact King Gumption on TikTok. Signed-in users can also download or delete their account data from the Account section in Studio.

Notice version: 17 November 2026.

Overlay names and settings are entered by you. Avoid placing private or sensitive personal information in them.

Live-stream viewer information

Top-liker and supporter overlays can receive display names, avatars, likes and gift information from a locally running live-event service on the same computer as your streaming software. King Gumption Studio keeps this recent activity in that browser's local storage for up to seven days. Viewer identities and activity totals are not uploaded to the King Gumption cloud account API by these overlays. Gift discovery uploads only gift metadata such as gift ID, name, coin amount and image location.

The streamer remains responsible for using viewer information fairly and for any notices required on their stream or channel.

Who receives information

  • Cloudflare provides the API, D1 database, R2 artwork/audio storage and related infrastructure.
  • Resend is the prepared transactional-email provider. Once configured, approved invitations and account-security messages include your email address and necessary account links. Delivery is currently disabled while the sender is being configured. No Gmail password is requested or stored.
  • Public gift-catalogue sources and TikTok image CDNs provide catalogue metadata and artwork fetched by the service. Your private saved design configuration is not sent to catalogue providers.
  • Cloudflare Workers Static Assets hosts kinggumption.com and processes technical requests to deliver the website.
  • Google Fonts supplies website fonts and receives the technical request needed to deliver them.
  • TikTok and live-event services selected by the streamer are separate services and operate under their own privacy terms.
  • Ko-fi, Twitch and YouTube receive information only if you choose to follow one of the external support or watch links.

Some providers may process information outside the UK. We rely on the relevant provider terms and contractual transfer safeguards. Cloudflare's Data Processing Addendum includes UK and EU transfer mechanisms.

Canvas links

Anyone with a Canvas link can load its name, dimensions and overlay configuration. Keep the link private. To revoke it, open Canvas Designer and choose Replace Link, or delete the Canvas.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing. Studio provides the following controls while signed in:

  • Download a JSON copy of account and cloud overlay data.
  • Request a verified email change after confirming the password, once email delivery is enabled. Email recovery is currently unavailable because a sender is not configured.
  • Clear editor and live-activity data stored in the current browser.
  • Permanently delete the account, saved versions, revision history and output URLs.

You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Security and changes

We use salted password hashing, short-lived sessions, access controls, rate limiting, encrypted cloud storage and transport encryption. Direct links, canvas URLs and hosted audio URLs contain capability tokens. Anyone with one of these URLs can access that resource. Keep them private; you can replace a design’s Direct link or a canvas URL without deleting the design. Indexing directives discourage search indexing but are not access controls. Account passwords should also be kept private. Local database/source backups used for releases are kept on the operator’s computer and are not published; operator backups may retain historical account data until manually removed. Deletion requests can be sent to the privacy contact for backup handling, and deleted account data must not be restored into active use.

We will update this page when processing or retention changes materially and show the current notice date above.

Session-based overlays

When a compact live graphic is enabled, its private source link can submit normalized live events to keep its instances in sync. Transient session data can include viewer names, avatar URLs, recent event IDs and totals. This state expires after 24 hours and is removed when the design or account is deleted. Signed-in account access is required to start, reset or adjust a session. Keep source links private.

Shared TikTok LIVE connection

When you connect a TikTok username in Studio, we save that username and your connection preference in Cloudflare Durable Object storage until you change it or delete your account. Euler Stream receives the selected public username and supplies public LIVE events. Our server relays the viewer names, public avatar URLs, gift details and engagement counts needed by your overlays. Each account has a separate relay, shared by its Direct URLs and Canvases. The Euler credential stays on the server and is never included in source URLs. Raw event history is not recorded by this relay; a bounded in-memory list of recent event IDs prevents duplicate delivery. Compact graphic session state retains the 24-hour expiry described above.
