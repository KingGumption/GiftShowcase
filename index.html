Before you start

What is included?

Five overlay editors, saved designs, stable Direct links, canvases and version history. Browse the samples without an account. Sign in to save and publish. Hosted sounds have a 10 MiB account limit, with clips up to 30 seconds.

Do I need TikFinity?

You can design and preview without it. Live gifts, follows, likes and shares need TikFinity’s Event API running on the same computer as your streaming software.

Why might my streaming software ask about my local network?

The live widget connects to TikFinity on localhost:21213. Allow that connection only when you want live events. Browsing this site and using sample previews do not need access to your computer’s local services.

Direct link or Canvas?

A Direct link shows one saved design. A Canvas combines several designs in one Browser Source. Both follow saved changes; local drafts do not publish automatically unless you enable live editing.

Will all creator gifts be available?

Gift availability varies by region and creator. The catalogue updates automatically from available sources, and connected live widgets can discover gifts. Some creator gifts may still be missing. Get help with a missing gift.
